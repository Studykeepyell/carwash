Questions

Do I need to wait while you work?

No. Most customers drop the car off and we call or text when it's ready. Essential takes about 2–3 hours and Premium about 5–6.

What's the difference between the two packages?

Essential is a deep clean inside and out with a spray sealant. Premium adds paint decontamination, a machine polish, a longer-lasting sealant, and shampoo and extraction for carpets and seats.

Do you do ceramic coating or paint correction?

Yes, through our partner specialists. Request a quote and we'll match you with the right detailer and confirm the price before booking.

Can you detail in winter?

Yes. Because the work is done in our bay rather than a driveway, we detail year-round. Winter interiors with salt stains are a big part of what we do.
